# clinical_manifestations_of_cryoglobulinemia

- Type I:
  - hyperviscosity (cold worsens sx) →
    - HA,
    - visual Δ,
    - livedo,
    - digital ischemia
- Type II/III:
  - vasculitis (not affected by cold) → fever,
  - derm
    - (54–80%; purpura, livedo reticularis, ulcers),
  - arthralgia
    - (44–70%; symmetric migratory, small/med joints),
  - glomerulonephritis
    - (50%; MPGN),
  - neurologic (17–60%; peripheral neuropathy
    - (polyneuropathy > mononeuritis multiplex),
  - ↓ Hgb,
  - ↓ plt,
  - ↑ B-cell lymphoma risk,
  - GI (5%; pain, HSM, ↑ LFTs).
- [[meltzer’s_triad.md|Meltzer’s triad]]:
  - purpura,
  - arthralgias,
  - weakness in 25–30%.
