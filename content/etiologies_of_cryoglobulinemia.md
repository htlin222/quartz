# etiologies_of_cryoglobulinemia

- Hematologic diseases:
  - multiple myeloma,
  - MGUS,
  - Waldenström’s,
  - chronic lymphocytic leukemia in type I; B-cell lymphomas or solid-organ malignancies in type II
- Infxns (types II & III):
  - viral (HCV [>80% RNA ⊕], HBV, HIV, HAV, EBV, CMV),
  - bacterial (endocarditis, strep, etc.),
  - fungal (coccidiomycosis, etc.),
  - parasitic (malaria, amoebiasis)
- Autoimmune syndromes (type III >II):
  - Sjögren’s syndrome,
  - SLE,
  - RA,
  - PAN
- Renal transplant recipients (Clin Nephrol 2008;69:239)
