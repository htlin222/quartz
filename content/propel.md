# propel

- (Pralatrexate in Patients with Relapsed or Refractory Peripheral T-Cell
  Lymphoma)

- 30 mg/m2/wk for 6 weeks in 7-week cycles.

- The response rate in 109 evaluable patients was

  - 29% (32 of 109),
    - including 12 complete responses (11%) and
    - 20 partial responses (18%), with a median DoR of 10.1 months.

- Median PFS and OS were

  - 3.5 And 14.5 Months, Respectively.

- The Most Common Grade 3/4 Adverse Events Were Thrombocytopenia (32%), Mucositis (22%), Neutropenia (22%), And Anemia (18%).

> O’Connor OA. Pralatrexate in Patients With Relapsed or Refractory Peripheral T-Cell Lymphoma: Results From the Pivotal PROPEL Study. Journal of Clinical Oncology. Published online March 20, 2011.
