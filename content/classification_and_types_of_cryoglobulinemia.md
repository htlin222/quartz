# classification_and_types_of_cryoglobulinemia


- Classification system by Brouet et al. in 1974 based on isotype(s) of immunoglobulins
    - Type I: Monoclonal immunoglobulin (IgM, IgG, or IgA)
    - Type II: Polyclonal IgG plus monoclonal IgM with rheumatoid-factor
    activity
    - Type III: Polyclonal IgG, polyclonal IgM, or both; mixed cryoglobulinemias
