# work_in_adult_all

- start diagnostic workup for adult acute lymphoblastic leukemia
  Morphology

- specifically:
  - Morphology
    - Defines infiltration (>25% required for a diagnosis of ALL vs lymphoblastic lymphoma)
    - recngnize L3 (Burkitt-type) subsets
  - Multicolor Flow cytometry ([[mfc.md|MFC]])
    - B-lineage ALL (B-LIN): CD19, cCD79a, c/sCD22 (minimal requirement), TdT, CD10, CD20, cIgM, sIg (kappa or lambda)
    - [[egil_classification.md|EGIL classification]]
      - Pro-B: CD19/CD79a/cCD22+/CD10– (B-I), NG2
      - Common: CD10+/cIgM– (B-II)
      - Pre-B: cIgM+/sIg– (B-III)
    - T-lineage ALL∗: c/sCD3, CD7 (minimal requirement), TdT, CD1a, CD2, CD5, CD4, CD8, TCR α/β or γ/δ
      - Pro-T: cCD3/CD7+ (T-I)
      - Pre-T: CD2/CD5+ (T-II)
      - Cortical-T: CD1a+ (T-III)
      - Mature-T: sCD3+/CD1a– (T-IV)
  - MPO: r/o AML
  - Molecular genetics
    - t(9;22)/Ph+/BCR::ABL1
    - t(4;11)+/KMT2Ar
    - t(1;19)+/TCF3::PBX1
    - Other high-risk cytogenetics
  - MRD (molecular or MFC)
  - consider extended genomics: NGS
